# Ihsan — Privacy Policy

*Effective 5 September 2026 · Sameer Studios LLC*

This is the same policy the app shows under Set → About → Privacy
policy. Host this file (or its HTML) and enter the URL in the App Store
Connect privacy policy field; the in-app copy lives in
`ihsan/Settings/PrivacyPolicyScreen.swift` and the two must say the same
thing.

## The short version

Ihsan collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, and no server
run by the developer. What you record stays on your devices and in your
own iCloud.

## What Ihsan keeps

Your prayer log, reflections, voice memos, settings, and any makeup or
fasting ledgers live in a database on this device. When iCloud is signed
in, eligible records sync to your private iCloud database, which only
your own devices can read.

A voice recording stays on the device that made it. Only its transcript
and date travel with your other records.

## Location

Your location is used to compute prayer times, the qibla, and nearby
masjids. Coordinates are used in the moment and are never written to
storage. Only your city and country are saved, for display.

Location is read while the app is open. Ihsan never asks for background
access and does not know where you have been.

If you set a masjid as yours, its position is kept so the app can show
its times. That is the one coordinate in the store, and removing your
masjid removes it.

## What leaves your device

Ihsan makes four kinds of requests. Three go to Apple: reverse
geocoding turns coordinates into a city name; the nearby-masjid search
sends your position to Apple Maps only while that sheet is open; a
weather reading, only while something in the app uses it: the living
sky (Set → Display, off by default) or the weather duas (Set → Adhkār,
on by default). The fourth fetches a small file of prayer thresholds and
reflection prompts from the developer's public GitHub repository; the
same file ships inside the app, so nothing depends on it.

None of these requests carries anything that identifies you, and nothing
is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

## Microphone and speech

Recording a reflection uses the microphone. Transcription runs on this
device with Apple's speech recognition; no audio leaves your phone.

## Notifications and alarms

Prayer notifications and wake alarms are scheduled on this device.
Nothing is sent from a server, and every one of them is yours to switch
off.

## Your data, your choice

Set → Privacy exports your prayer log and reflections as a file you
own, and deletes everything with one confirmation. Removing Ihsan from a device removes
its local data; what is in iCloud can be removed from iCloud settings.

## Changes and contact

This policy describes Ihsan 1.0. If it changes, the new version ships
with the app and is dated here. Questions can reach the developer
through Ihsan's App Store page.
