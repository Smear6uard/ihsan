# PATH READOUT — FIQH REVIEW REQUEST

**Status: DRAFT — PENDING SCHOLAR REVIEW.**

This document lists the complete cited copy that the Path screen's
period readout is able to display. The source of truth is
`Packages/IhsanFiqhConfig/Sources/IhsanFiqhConfig/PathFindingGrounding.swift`;
`PathFiqhReviewArtifactTests` holds the two together, so copy that
ships without appearing here fails the build, and copy edited here
without being edited in the source fails it too.

## What is being asked

For each of the nine readings below, please check:

1. **Attribution** — is the report correctly attributed, and is the
   collection and number right?
2. **Paraphrase** — the reports are given in indirect speech rather
   than as quotations. Is each paraphrase faithful?
3. **Application** — the reading is triggered by the user's own logs
   (the condition is stated under each). Is it fair to show this text
   to a person whose ledger looks like that?
4. **Restraint** — none of this may function as a ruling on the person.
   Does any of it read as one?

## How the app uses this

- The **finding** above each of these is arithmetic over the user's own
  prayer log — a count, never a judgement — and is generated by
  `PathFinding` in the app target, not by any model.
- The on-device model (Foundation Models) is **prohibited from
  religious content entirely** by `InsightSystemInstructions`, and its
  output is filtered by `InsightContentFilter` before display. It never
  produces, edits, or selects any of the text below.
- The text below appears only when the user expands **FIQH CONTEXT** on
  the card. It is collapsed by default.
- Exactly one reading is shown at a time — the first whose condition
  the ledger satisfies, in the order listed.
- A remote fiqh config may replace any entry, but an entry missing a
  title, body, or citation is refused in favour of the text here.

## Editorial decisions taken (please confirm or correct)

- **Honorific.** Following the convention already set in
  `ADHKAR_REVIEW.md`, the editorial ﷺ is not inserted after the
  Prophet's name.
- **Indirect speech.** Nothing is presented as a quotation, and the
  card carries no quotation marks, so that no English rendering is
  mistaken for transmitted wording.
- **Disputed points are named as disputed.** Where the schools differ
  (order and urgency of make-up prayers; whether congregation is
  obligatory) the copy says so rather than picking a position.
- **Variant narrations are named.** The congregation multiplier is
  given as twenty-seven with the twenty-five variant noted.
- **ʿAṣr's two reports are paired.** The warning (Bukhārī 552) is never
  shown without the promise (Bukhārī 574).

## 1. `unrecordedSlots`

**Shown when:** more than half of the elapsed, non-paused prayer slots
in the period carry no record at all.

**Title:** An empty slot is not a missed prayer

**Body:** Ihsan keeps the two apart on purpose. A slot with no record
means nothing was entered and the app does not know what happened.
Missed means you told it the prayer was not prayed inside its window.
Prayer is fixed on the believers at appointed times, so each slot is a
real appointment — but only the ones you fill in can tell you anything.

**Citation:** Qur’an 4:103

---

## 2. `outstandingMakeups`

**Shown when:** the period contains a prayer logged missed with no qadāʾ
log linked to it.

**Title:** A missed prayer is repaid by praying it

**Body:** Nothing stands in for it — not ṣadaqah, not extra voluntary
prayers. The Prophet said that whoever forgets a prayer or sleeps
through it should pray it when he remembers, and that there is no
expiation for it except that, then recited: establish prayer for My
remembrance. The schools differ over the order and the urgency. They do
not differ that it is still owed.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 597 · Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 684 · Qur’an 20:14

---

## 3. `weakFajrOrIsha`

**Shown when:** Fajr or ʿIshāʾ is prayed on the fewest days of the five,
on at most half the elapsed days, and at least 25 percentage points
below the most-prayed prayer.

**Title:** These are the two that thin out first

**Body:** It is named directly. The Prophet said no prayer is heavier
upon the hypocrites than ʿIshāʾ and Fajr, and that if they knew what was
in them they would come even if they had to crawl. The point is not the
verdict, it is the diagnosis: these two sit at the edges of sleep, and
that is where a record thins first for almost everyone.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 657

---

## 4. `weakAsr`

**Shown when:** ʿAṣr meets the same condition as above.

**Title:** ʿAṣr carries an explicit warning

**Body:** Whoever misses ʿAṣr, the Prophet said, is like one who lost
his family and property. The same ʿAṣr is one of the two cool prayers,
and whoever prays both of them enters Paradise. It falls in the working
part of the afternoon, which is exactly why it is the one that gets
pushed.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 552 · Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 574

---

## 5. `weakOtherPrayer`

**Shown when:** Ẓuhr or Maghrib meets the same condition as above.

**Title:** At its time, not merely inside it

**Body:** Asked which deed is most beloved to Allah, the Prophet
answered: the prayer at its time. Not somewhere in the window — at its
time. Maghrib in particular has the narrowest window of the five, so it
is the one most often lost to something that looked like it would take
five minutes.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 527

---

## 6. `delayedAsr`

**Shown when:** ʿAṣr is logged Delayed on at least three elapsed days
and on more days than it is logged On Time. Note that this reading is
about a prayer that IS being prayed, every day, at the end of its
window.

**Title:** The prayer of the one who watches the sun

**Body:** Anas ibn Mālik described the hypocrite’s ʿAṣr: he sits
watching the sun until it sits between the horns of Shayṭān, then gets
up and pecks out four rakʿahs with barely any remembrance of Allah in
them. A prayer at the edge of its window is valid and it counts. The
report is about making the edge your habit.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim 622

---

## 7. `delayedOtherPrayer`

**Shown when:** any other prayer meets the same condition.

**Title:** Valid, and still at the edge

**Body:** A prayer performed in the last part of its window counts in
full. Ihsan records it as Delayed only so it stays distinguishable from
a prayer taken at its time — which is the thing the Prophet named as
the deed most beloved to Allah. It is a distinction worth being able to
see, not a separate ruling.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 527 · Qur’an 4:103

---

## 8. `noJamaah`

**Shown when:** at least ten prayers are recorded in the period and none
of them is marked as prayed in congregation.

**Title:** Twenty-seven to one

**Body:** The Prophet said the prayer in congregation is twenty-seven
times superior to the prayer prayed alone; other sound narrations give
the figure as twenty-five. Either way it is the largest single
multiplier available on something already being done five times a day.
The schools differ on whether congregation is obligatory. They do not
differ on the reward.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 645

---

## 9. `steady`

**Shown when:** none of the above applies.

**Title:** Small and constant beats large and occasional

**Body:** ʿĀʾishah reported that the deeds most beloved to Allah are the
ones done most constantly, even when they are few. A record that holds
is worth more than one that spikes. So the useful question here is not
more prayers — it is the same prayers, with other people.

**Citation:** Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 6464
